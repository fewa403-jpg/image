Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how this website ("we," "us," the "Service") handles information when you use our free online image editing tools.

1. Images you upload

Most editing tools on this site — crop, filters, text, stickers, layers, Style Match, Enhance, Cutout — run entirely in your own browser. Your photo never leaves your device for these features.

The "Remove Background (AI)" tool sends your photo to our own server for processing (not a third-party AI service), where it is used only to generate the result and is not stored, logged, or used for any other purpose. The "Remove Object" tool sends your photo to a third-party AI provider (Hugging Face) solely to perform that specific edit.

2. Information we don't collect

We don't require an account, and we don't ask for your name, email, or payment details to use the editing tools.

3. Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party providers, such as Google AdSense. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad preferences at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

4. Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and access times) for security and troubleshooting purposes. This data is not used to identify individuals.

5. Third-party services

This site is hosted on Render and uses open-source libraries (such as Fabric.js, TensorFlow.js, and onnxruntime) that run in your browser or on our server solely to provide editing functionality.

6. Children's privacy

This Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at youremail@example.com.
